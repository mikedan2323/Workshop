Welcome to the Pathfinder Counsellors Workshop

Introduction

Welcome to our Pathfinder Counsellors Workshop! This session is designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills needed to effectively serve as counsellors in our Pathfinder program.

As counsellors, you play a crucial role in the spiritual and personal development of our Pathfinders. Today, we'll explore the vision for the year, understand the Pathfinder class structure, and define the various roles and responsibilities.

Workshop Agenda:

  • Welcome and Prayer
  • Vision for the Year
  • Pathfinder Class Structure
  • Role Responsibilities Overview
  • Interactive Role Scenarios
  • Questions & Answers
  • Role Assignments & Closing Prayer

Key Goals:

4

Counsellor Best Practices

Spiritual Leadership

  • Model Christian values in all interactions
  • Begin activities with prayer
  • Share personal testimonies when appropriate
  • Connect Pathfinder activities to biblical principles
  • Encourage spiritual growth & discussion

Effective Communication

  • Provide clear, concise, and consistent instructions
  • Practice active listening with Pathfinders & parents
  • Use age-appropriate language
  • Offer regular, constructive feedback
  • Maintain open communication with fellow leaders

Safety Consciousness

  • Always maintain proper supervision ratios
  • Know and follow emergency procedures
  • Conduct risk assessments for activities
  • Practice preventative measures (first aid ready, etc.)
  • Model safe behavior in all situations

Interactive Session: Role Scenarios

During our workshop, we'll break into small groups to work through the following role-specific challenges:

1

Uniform Issue

A Pathfinder consistently arrives without proper uniform components. How would you address this while being supportive and understanding potential barriers?

2

Disengagement

You notice a Pathfinder seems withdrawn and uninterested in activities they usually enjoy. What steps would you take to connect and understand?

3

Parent Concern

Parents express concern about the difficulty level or time commitment of an upcoming honor. How would you address their concerns collaboratively?

4

New Pathfinder Integration

A new Pathfinder joins mid-year and is struggling to catch up on requirements and integrate socially. What support plan would you implement?
